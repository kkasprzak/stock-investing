## What this is

A **reconstruction** of what was public on the evening of 21 Sep 2026 — after RB 50/2026 (18:25) and
before RB 51/2026 (22 Sep, 16:52). It reproduces no run; it is a **control**: with the facts laid
out, does the skill call Phase I completed and find no current programme?

Included: RB 24/2026, RB 26/2026, every weekly transaction report published by then, RB 48/2026 and
RB 50/2026. RB 49/2026 (18:21) is in — it was published four minutes before RB 50/2026.

ESPI files are each report's **full page text** as fetched on 2026-10-04, ending at the
signatures; attachments (PDFs) are not included, as a page fetch would not return them. Unlike the
replay inputs, these are the documents themselves rather than model-written summaries — easier to
read than anything a live run receives.
