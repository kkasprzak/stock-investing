# Allegro.eu Share Buyback Program Details

**Publication Date:** June 25, 2026

**Program Overview:**
Allegro.eu's Board of Directors approved a share buyback initiative with the following Phase I parameters:

- **Shares to Acquire:** Up to 42,105,263 shares
- **Maximum Price Per Share:** 45 zloty
- **Total Budget:** Not exceeding 800 million zloty
- **Executing Broker:** Erste Bank Polska - Erste Biuro Maklerskie
- **Start Date:** No earlier than July 15, 2026
- **End Date:** No later than June 25, 2027

The company stated that "Allegro.eu przyjęło program skupu akcji własnych" (Allegro.eu adopted a share buyback program), marking a structured approach to repurchasing its own equity over a twelve-month window.
