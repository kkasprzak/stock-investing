## Allegro.eu Share Buyback Programme - November 2025 Announcement

The Board of Directors authorized a new share repurchase program on November 18, 2025, permitting buybacks of up to 4,664,941 shares, with a total cap of 209.92 million PLN and a maximum purchase price of 45 PLN per share, running through May 31, 2026.

### Program Details:

- **Authorization Date:** November 18, 2025
- **Number of Shares:** Up to 4,664,941 shares for a total of PLN 209.92 million
- **Maximum Price:** PLN 45 per share
- **Program Duration:** Valid until May 31, 2026
- **Purpose:** These shares are designated to support an employee incentive plan

The buyback programme was announced through an official resolution by Allegro.eu's Board of Directors to facilitate employee share awards and meet obligations under the company's employee incentive plan.
