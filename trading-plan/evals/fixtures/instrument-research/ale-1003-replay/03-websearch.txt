## Program Overview

Allegro.eu's Board of Directors approved the first phase of a share buyback program for cancellation purposes, with the company able to allocate up to 800 million PLN to acquire shares at a maximum of 45 PLN per share.

## Phase I Details

Allegro completed Phase I of the share buyback program, acquiring 17,879,330 shares (1.76% of share capital), with total transaction value of 800 million PLN, executed between July 15 and September 21, 2026, at an average purchase price of 44.74 PLN per share.

## Price Adjustments

The Board of Directors increased the maximum purchase price for Phase I from 45 PLN to 50 PLN per share. Subsequently, the Board proposed further increasing the maximum purchase price to 65 PLN per share, with shareholder decision scheduled for a general meeting on November 18, 2026.

## Phase II

In Phase II, the company can acquire a maximum of 42,105,263 shares at a price not exceeding 50 PLN per share, with Phase II starting on September 23, 2026 and ending no later than June 25, 2027.
